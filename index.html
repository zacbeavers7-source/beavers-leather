THE STORY BEHIND THE LEATHER

More Than Leather

I first got into leatherwork during my time in the military while training to become a survival specialist. What started as a useful skill became something I genuinely enjoyed, and I've now been working with leather for more than eight years.

For me, leatherwork is more than making something functional. It's my way to slow down, focus, and create.

The best part is taking an idea that exists only in someone's head and turning it into something they can actually hold.

Whether it's a holster, belt, sheath, bag, piece of armor, or something completely different, I put my full effort into bringing that idea to life as closely as possible to the way you imagined it.

You imagine it. I build it.
